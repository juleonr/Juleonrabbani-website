About

Juleon Rabbani, DrPH, MPH is the Director of the Biostatistical Consulting Unit (BSCU) and a Research Scientist in Biostatistics within the Kaiser Permanente Division of Research (DOR) in Northern California. Since 2023, he has directed a research portfolio exceeding $16 million, leading a team of more than 30 biostatisticians, analysts, and program staff.

His team supports 400+ active clinical research projects and generates over 150 peer-reviewed publications and presentations each year — work that advances Kaiser Permanente's learning health system, supports GME accreditation, strengthens community health commitments, and builds evidence across clinical specialties. He serves as a member of the Kaiser Permanente Research Northern California (KPRNC) Executive Council.

As a principal and co-investigator, Dr. Rabbani has led institutionally funded studies spanning women's health, emergency medicine, psychiatry, ophthalmology, cancer epidemiology, advance care planning, and population health. He completed his public health training with the U.S. Department of Defense and Public Health Service in Bethesda, MD, and previously directed research activities for multiple physician training programs at the Kaiser Permanente Vallejo Medical Center, including founding the KP Public Health Research Internship Program in 2017.

Biostatistical Consulting Unit

The BSCU at Kaiser Permanente Northern California supports the full continuum of clinical scholarship — from medical students completing their first research project to independent attending physicians advancing their specialty.

GME Trainee Support
Embedded biostatistical consultation for residents and fellows across KPNC GME programs. Supports scholarly project design, data analysis, manuscript preparation, and conference presentation.
Medical Student Research
Consultative and mentorship support for KPSOM students and visiting medical students pursuing clinical and health services research under KPNC physician faculty.
Attending Physician Consulting
Full-service biostatistical partnership for TPMG attending physicians — from research question formulation through submission-ready analyses and publication support.
Community Health & TPMG Initiatives
Collaborative research support for Community Health programs and TPMG quality and outcomes work, with a focus on health equity, population-level interventions, and program evaluation.
